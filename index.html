About

I am a postdoctoral scholar in the Center for Cryptographic Research in the Mathematics and Statistics Department at the University of South Florida in Tampa, hosted by Jean-François Biasse. I am broadly interested in theoretical computer science and its mathematical foundations. I am especially interested in the foundations of cryptography, computational complexity, and the connections between error-correcting codes and geometric lattices.
